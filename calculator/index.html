Mixing order & safety

Add water first, then SH, then a bleach-stable surfactant. Never mix sodium hypochlorite with acids, ammonia, F9 BARC, or other incompatible cleaners. Use separate labeled sprayers, PPE, and ventilation.

Do not automatically sell work outside current safe access/equipment limits. High, steep, complex, or two-story roof treatments require separate judgment until the dedicated softwash system is in service. Dryer vents beyond 40 ft or inaccessible terminations require approval or referral.

✓ Final rinse / plant rinse
✓ Verify drainage / cleanup
✓ Take after photos
✓ Customer walkthrough when practical
✓ Collect remaining balance

Do not mix

Never combine SH with acids, ammonia, F9 BARC, or unknown/incompatible chemistry. Flush and label equipment appropriately.
