Field Safety Card

🧤 PPE first

Use eye, skin, respiratory and footwear protection required by the label and SDS.

🚫 Never mix unknown products

Keep SH, acids, ammonia and specialty cleaners separated in labeled sprayers.

🌿 Control overspray and runoff

Move people and pets, protect plants and metals, and follow local wastewater requirements.

📄 The label controls

This calculator is a planning aid. The current container label and SDS override every preset in the app.

Quick safety order

  1. Read the current product label and SDS.
  2. Wear eye/skin protection and keep people, pets, and plants clear.
  3. Add water first, then SH, then bleach-stable surfactant.
  4. Use separate labeled sprayers for acid, alkaline, and SH products.
  5. Pre-wet and post-rinse plants; never let mix dry on a surface.
  6. Test an inconspicuous spot and start weaker when uncertain.
  7. Rinse tools and do not seal or store mixed SH long-term.
