SERVICE TERMS

Clear expectations before the work starts.

These general terms support the written estimate for First In Response Exteriors. The scope, price, and property-specific notes in an accepted estimate control the actual job.

Important: Property-specific scope, exclusions, pricing, access notes, and other written estimate terms take priority over general website descriptions.

Estimates and scope

Website estimator results are preliminary and are not a final contract price. Final pricing is confirmed after the property, measurements, access, surface condition, and requested services are reviewed. Work is limited to the services and areas included in the accepted estimate.

Scheduling and deposit

A 50% deposit is required to reserve approved work on the schedule. The remaining balance and accepted payment method are handled according to the approved estimate or invoice.

Access and safety

Services are performed only where access and conditions can be handled safely with the available equipment. Height, roof pitch, electrical hazards, unstable surfaces, inaccessible areas, weather, or other site conditions may limit or change the work that can safely be completed.

Surface condition and results

Cleaning removes or treats appropriate exterior buildup; it does not repair pre-existing damage, failed coatings, oxidation, deterioration, leaks, broken drainage, or other underlying defects. Some stains or discoloration may not be completely removable.

Specialty services

Gutter brightening, oxidation removal, hard-water stain removal, rust treatment, underground drain flushing, dryer-vent work, and similar specialty services may require separate evaluation, processes, limitations, or pricing.

Customer responsibilities

Customers should disclose known property concerns and provide reasonable access to the approved work areas. Vehicles, fragile items, open windows, pets, and other items that could interfere with exterior cleaning should be secured or moved when requested before service.

Questions

Questions about a specific estimate or scheduled job should be addressed before work begins by calling or texting (918) 922-9366 or emailing kyle@firstinresponseexteriors.com.
