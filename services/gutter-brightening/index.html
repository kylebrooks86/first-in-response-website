What exterior gutter brightening is designed to treat

Gutter cleaning and gutter brightening solve different problems

Gutter cleaning is functional maintenance: debris is removed from the channel, accessible downspouts are flushed, and normal flow is checked. Gutter brightening is cosmetic: the exterior face is treated for visible streaking and residue. One service does not automatically include the other.

Some discoloration may be permanent

Tiger striping and exterior residue can often improve, but permanent fading, damaged coatings, corrosion, deep oxidation, and age-related finish changes may remain. Kyle reviews the condition and sets realistic expectations before treatment.

Gutter brightening questions

What photos help with a gutter brightening estimate?

Send your property address and photos showing the stained gutter faces, height, and access around the home. Include a close-up of the streaks or damaged finish. Say whether you also need debris removed from inside the gutters, since gutter cleanout and exterior brightening are separate services.

Is gutter brightening included with gutter cleaning?

No. Gutter cleaning clears the interior channel and checks downspout flow. Gutter brightening is a separate cosmetic treatment for the outside face of the gutter.

What are tiger stripes on gutters?

Tiger stripes are dark vertical streaks that can form as runoff, dirt, organic buildup, and oxidation-related residue travel down the exterior gutter face.

Will every streak disappear completely?

Not always. Results depend on the gutter finish, age, staining, oxidation, prior damage, and how long the discoloration has been present. Permanent fading, corrosion, or coating damage may remain.

How is gutter brightening priced?

Gutter brightening is $2.00 per linear foot. The $150 minimum service charge applies to a scheduled visit, and Kyle confirms the final scope in writing.

Can I schedule gutter cleaning and brightening together?

Yes. The functional cleanout and cosmetic brightening can be included in the same written estimate, while remaining separate line items so the scope is clear.
