## Website-wide rules

- Minimum job charge: **$150**
- Deposit required to reserve approved work on the schedule: **50%**
- Remaining balance due: **Upon completion of the work**
- First Responder & Military Appreciation Discount: **5%**
- Existing gutter guards may be removed/reinstalled when required; FIRE does **not** sell or install new gutter guards.
- Bundle discounts may be offered where appropriate.
- Customer-facing business name: **First In Response Exteriors**.
- Positioning: **firefighter-owned and insured**. Do not describe the company as veteran-owned.

## Services that require property review

Do not invent fixed website rates for services that have not been assigned a current locked price. Examples include roof cleaning, premium trim work, pavers, decks, premium fence restoration, patio furniture, condenser cleaning, deep window frame/sill cleaning, oxidation removal, hard-water removal, rust removal, trash-bin cleaning, dryer vents, underground drain/French-drain flushing, and most vehicle/boat/trailer services.

For these services, collect the property/address, photos, quantities where useful, access details, and condition before confirming final pricing.

## Equipment/access limits that affect estimates

- Roof work: current setup is best suited to safely accessible low-rise work; unsafe/high/complex access may be declined.
- Dryer vent tool reach: approximately 40 ft.
- Drain jetter: approximately 100 ft.
- Inspection camera: approximately 16 ft.

## Pricing-change rule

When a rate changes, update both this file and every customer-facing location that uses the rate, especially `index.html` estimator logic and the matching service page. Re-test the estimator before production deployment.
