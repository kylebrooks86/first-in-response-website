# Deliberate portable customer recovery — isolated candidates

The Customers recovery workflow now exposes a deliberate recovery form only after successful portable file/account/target validation and an advisory empty-destination preflight. It requires a fresh phrase and explicit checkbox approval. The encrypted verified envelope and approval are consumed by each attempt; a failure requires another explicit preflight. No automatic unlock, merge, overwrite, discard or retry is added.

The repository permits recovery only while locked and requires a literal confirmation. The existing vault validates/decrypts the complete bundle and reencrypts all rows before one add-only native IndexedDB transaction; its occupancy cursor is authoritative even if another writer occupied the destination after preflight. Any sealed row blocks recovery. Controller identity checks and normal teardown revoke pending work on logout; late success is not published. A Lock/Stop control remains available while work is pending. Completion stays locked and updates the candidate setup panel; the owner must unlock to inspect and re-export/verify. Interrupted or ambiguous failures advise inspection instead of claiming that no commit could have occurred.
