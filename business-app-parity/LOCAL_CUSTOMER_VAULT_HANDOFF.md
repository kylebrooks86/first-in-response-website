The separate account/target vault namespace stores one encrypted, schema-validated customer snapshot. Initialization encrypts before opening a native write transaction and uses add, never put, so existing snapshots cannot be overwritten. Failed/aborted initialization preserves existing state. Unlock requires the exact recovery phrase and validates decrypted customer data before exposing detached read-only records. Sessions hold no saved passphrase. Session lock clears its snapshot reference; handle lock revokes its sessions and prevents pending encryption/decryption from publishing after lock. Close/version change revokes the handle. No plaintext business records are written by this vault.
