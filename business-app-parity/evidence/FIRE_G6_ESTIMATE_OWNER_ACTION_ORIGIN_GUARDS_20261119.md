## Concrete original vulnerabilities closed

The owner estimate `PATCH /api/estimates/[id]` previously directly called `request.json()` before applying the existing same-origin/JSON request restrictions already deployed elsewhere in source. An authenticated browser request with a forged cross-origin invocation could enter the pricing/scheduling write path.

Three additional existing owner actions lacked the same browser-origin gate: `PATCH /api/estimates/[id]/change-requests` (permanent resolution audit), `POST /api/estimates/[id]/share` (private bearer link generation), and `POST /api/estimates/[id]/void` (status transition to declined). Their owner-authentication and business-state rules were retained and not widened.
