## Targeted coding freeze and regression policy

- No rebuild, no unsolicited feature additions, and no general aesthetic cycles. Preserve existing approved FIRE layout, service pricing/rate discretion, stacked promotions when eligible, 50% deposit process, Google Voice business-number-first workflow, and personal fallback warnings.
- Source PR #79 adds draft-protection and navigation checks. Every new code commit needs **exact-head** checks; previous green checks are not transferable to a newer SHA.
- An initial browser test on the preceding PR #79 head failed the final service option tap in **667×375 landscape** but its targeted retry passed without code change. Treat this as a short-device watchpoint; do not silently mark a physical-device PASS.
- Keep permanent Customer #/Job # flags OFF until audited additive migration, complete historical backfill, immutable links and numbered-v2 lossless archive + photo restore are approved for each target. Existing legacy backup's fail-closed numbered-record 409 must remain in place.
- Customer-save/uncertain-send operations must not automatically retry destructive writes; verify immutable receipt/readback before communicating success.

1. **No new publication until preflight.** Identify the intended QA Worker/version, source SHA and artifact; verify serving version, 100% allocation, exact QA D1/R2 bindings, backup/rollback receipts and no accidental Stripe or LIVE configuration.
